### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Software Engineer** and **AI Researcher** with a multi-disciplinary background spanning **Deep Learning, Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINN), Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks (KAN), Digital Twins, Cloud Solution Architecture, and Human-Centric UI/UX Design**.

- 🏛️ **AI Researcher** at **Beijing Institute of Technology** & **Wuhan University of Technology** (under supervision of *Dr. Muhammad Usman Shoukat*), authoring Q1 journal publications and IEEE conference papers.
- 🎓 **BS Software Engineering** graduate from **KFUEIT** (*CGPA: 3.68/4.00 · Class Rank 4th · Dean's Honor Award*).
- 🏆 **Triple Certified**: **AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate**, **Microsoft AI Agents Developer – Associate**, and **Google UX Design Professional**.
- 🔬 **Core Research Vectors**: *Agentic AI & Multilingual RAG, Battery Digital Twins (PINN-STGAT), Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks (KAN) for Autonomous Systems, Fiber Bragg Grating (FBG) Optical Sensing, Semi-Supervised Learning (SSL), and Hierarchical Edge Robotics Perception*.
